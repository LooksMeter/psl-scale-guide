# The PSL Scale: A Plain Guide

The PSL scale is a 0.25 to 8.0 rating system for facial attractiveness. It is stricter than the everyday 1 to 10 scale: an average face scores around 4, not 7.

This guide explains the tiers, what goes into a score and how to read one. It is maintained by [LooksMeter](https://www.looksmeter.com), which offers a [free PSL score](https://www.looksmeter.com/tools/psl-score) from a single photo.

## What PSL means

PSL is named after three online forums where the system developed: Puahate, Sluthate and Lookism. It does not stand for "personality, status, looks".

The scale rates the face only. It says nothing about body, style or character.

## The seven tiers

| Score | Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.25 - 1.5 | Very low | Severe asymmetry or very atypical proportions |
| 1.5 - 3.0 | Low Tier Normie (LTN) | Below-average facial harmony |
| 3.0 - 4.5 | Mid Tier Normie (MTN) | The statistical average; most faces land here |
| 4.5 - 5.5 | High Tier Normie (HTN) | Noticeably attractive, above average |
| 5.5 - 6.0 | Chadlite / Stacylite | Commercial-model territory |
| 6.0 - 7.0 | Chad / Stacy | Exceptionally rare structure and harmony |
| 7.0 - 8.0 | True Adam / True Eve | Theoretical ceiling; almost never seen unedited |

## How PSL compares with an everyday "out of 10"

PSL scores run from 0.25 to 8.0. People are used to casual "7 out of 10" ratings, which are inflated, so the same face gets a lower number on the PSL scale. This table shows roughly what each PSL score would be called in everyday terms.

| PSL score (0.25 to 8.0) | Everyday "out of 10" |
| --- | --- |
| 4.5 - 5.0 | 6.5 - 7.5 |
| 5.5 - 6.0 | 8 - 8.5 |
| 6.0 - 7.0 | 9 - 9.5+ |

## The four scoring categories

Each is scored on the same scale as the overall rating.

- **Harmony:** how well the features work together (facial thirds, fifths, overall balance).
- **Dimorphism:** how clearly masculine or feminine the facial structure reads.
- **Angularity:** definition of the jawline, cheekbones and chin.
- **Miscellaneous:** skin clarity, eyebrows, eye tilt, nose contour and hair framing.

The breakdown is more useful than the total. Angularity and much of Harmony are bone structure. Miscellaneous is where grooming, skincare and a haircut show up.

## Getting an accurate score

- Face the camera straight on, at eye level.
- Use even daylight, such as facing a window.
- Keep a neutral expression.
- No filters and no heavy editing.
- Stand back and crop; a phone held close distorts the face.

## Limits

A PSL score is an estimate from one image. It does not measure charm, voice, humour or kindness, and it is not medical or psychological advice. Different photos of the same person can score differently.
